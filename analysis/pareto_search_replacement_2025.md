## Reading the balanced design

The equal-weight compromise is candidate 1156: a 34.62% headline/business rate, a $20,000 zero bracket per schedule adult, a 24.23% middle rate through $80,000 per schedule adult, and the headline rate above that threshold. It combines a $0 adult nonrefundable credit, no refundable adult wage credit, a $8,000 refundable child credit, and a $3,500 adult / $0 child refundable health credit.

Its adult-relief architecture is zero bracket. The statutory zero bracket is $20,000 per schedule adult. The refundable wage credit has no earnings phase-in.

At the default assumptions, 91.0% of ESI-covered people are in non-losing tax units; the covered-person median annual resource change is $13,580, the 10th percentile is $531, and the mean absolute MTR change is 3.7 percentage points (67.6% remain within two points).

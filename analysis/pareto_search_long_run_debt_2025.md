## Reading the balanced design

The equal-weight compromise is candidate 266: a 40.02% headline/business rate, no statutory zero bracket, a 28.01% middle rate through $150,000 per schedule adult, and the headline rate above that threshold. It combines a $500 adult nonrefundable credit, no refundable adult wage credit, a $6,500 refundable child credit, and a $3,500 adult / $1,250 child refundable health credit.

Its adult-relief architecture is nonrefundable credit. The nonrefundable credit makes the effective zero-net-wage-tax threshold $1,785 per adult. The refundable wage credit has no earnings phase-in.

At the default assumptions, 68.4% of ESI-covered people are in non-losing tax units; the covered-person median annual resource change is $4,743, the 10th percentile is -$8,088, and the mean absolute MTR change is 6.3 percentage points (6.7% remain within two points).

The stress case uses 75% employer-health wage pass-through and raises the benchmark factor from 1.03 to 1.15. See the JSON for decile results, exact fiscal identities, scope limits, and the full frontier.

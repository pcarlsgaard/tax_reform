# Corrected X-tax Pareto search across fiscal targets

The same 20,000 seeded draws were evaluated against three fiscal constraints. The health credit is a true refundable premium-purchase credit, not an income exclusion. Its range is deliberately bounded at $0–$3,500 per ESI-covered adult and $0–$1,500 per covered child rather than allowed to reimburse the full individual-market benchmark premium.

The linked ESI microdata estimate that current employer and employee premium exclusions reduce combined federal income and payroll taxes by $449.4 billion, or $2,717 per covered person. The upper-bound adult/child split averages $3,039 across the modeled ESI population; the $3,000/$1,500 calibration point averages $2,654.

The full $956.0 billion modeled employer-contribution pool is redistributed as approximately $957.4 billion of cash wages to ESI policyholder workers. Household incidence includes the resulting resources received through a spouse.
